# Introduction to German Genealogy

## Historical Context is Key

Historical context teslls you where a record is located, why it exists and
what information it contains.

## Starting Information that One Needs

The four pieces of information are:

1. name
2. place
3. time
4. religion

## How to Discover You Ancestor's German Place of Origin

These are sources one should puruse:

1. obituaries
2. census
3. U.S. church German church registers
4. Cluster/FAN research
5. 1906 Naturalization Act
6. Hamburg Emigration Lists
7. CompGen's [Surname distribution map](https:///nvk.genealogy.net/map) 

## Vital and Civil Records

In the 1870s vital and civil records began.

## Research Process

1. Define the place of origin
2. Map the jurisdiction: parish, civil district, responsible archive
3. identify records
4. locate their source
5. decode and extract information in them
6. connect and expand the information
