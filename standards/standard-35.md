# Genealogy Standards

This is a consolidated Markdown table of the **freely accessible online
articles, posts, handouts, and presentations I found that either explicitly
discuss Standard 35 or substantially address the same source-analysis
principles**.

| Author | Article / Resource | Relationship to Standard 35 |
|---|---|---|
| Alice Hoyt Veen, CG | [Grains of Salt: Analyzing Sources, Information, and Evidence](https://bcgcertification.org/skillbuilding-grains-of-salt-analyzing-sources-information-and-evidence) | **Explicitly discusses Standard 35.** Especially useful on provenance, purpose, who authorized creation of a source, laws governing records, and the context in which records were created. |
| Angela Packer McGhie | [Studying Evidence Analysis, Part 1](https://genealogyeducation.blogspot.com/2016/02/studying-evidence-analysis-part-1.html) | **Explicitly identifies Standard 35 as the source-analysis standard** and discusses methods and additional readings for source and evidence analysis. |
| Angela Packer McGhie, CG | [Document Analysis: Digging into the Details](https://www.familysearch.org/en/rootstech/session/document-analysis-digging-into-the-details) | Closely addresses Standard 35: background analysis, record characteristics, legal terminology, informants, and factors affecting a document's reliability. Free video and syllabus. |
| Shannon Green | [From Questionable to Credible: Analyzing and Verifying Genealogical Sources](https://www.familysearch.org/en/rootstech/session/from-questionable-to-credible-analyzing-and-verifying-genealogical-sources) | Source-analysis methodology corresponding closely to Standard 35, including assessing the origins and characteristics of sources and evaluating their credibility. Free video and syllabus. |
| Judy G. Russell, JD, CG, CGL | [From Statutes to Stories: Finding the Law for Family History](https://familytreewebinars.com/webinar/from-statutes-to-stories-finding-the-law-for-family-history) | Particularly relevant to the **context/governance** portion of Standard 35: determining what law caused a record to be created, what officials were required to record, and why a record exists. |
| Michael Grant Hait Jr., CG | [Reporting Research in Progress](https://www.bcgcertification.org/skillbuilding-reporting-research-in-progress) | BCG material that invokes Standard 35 in connection with properly evaluating and explaining sources and their characteristics. BCG also identifies this article as a resource for its research standards. |
| Nicole Gilkison LaRue, CG | [Their Mark Here: Signatures and Marks as Identifying Tools](https://familytreewebinars.com/media/their-mark-here-legacy/?post_id=47381&type=syllabus) | Uses source-analysis principles associated with Standard 35, particularly close examination of a document's physical and evidentiary characteristics. |
| — | [Uncovering Ancestral Origins](https://cms-z-assets.familysearch.org/8e/55/9292ce8f40676ca285b765c49a14/uncovering-ancestral-origins-oconnell.pdf) | Includes discussion of **Standard 35, Source Analysis**, together with related information-analysis principles. |
